机器人关节与机械臂

产品课程 01 · 8 句精读 · 核心部件与产品优势

完整音频

完整英文原文

From twenty eighteen to twenty twenty-one, we spent approximately three and a half years refining our products.

We developed the key components of our robotic joints in-house, including harmonic reducers, servo motors, drives, and controllers.

For the same joint size, we can deliver higher torque.

For the same torque output, our joint can be more compact.

This allows us to build robotic arms that are slim, lightweight, and similar in appearance to a human arm.

Our robotic arm weighs only seven point two kilograms, but provides a payload of five kilograms.

It can operate reliably and stably.

According to our test results, it has achieved an M T B F of fifty million hours.

完整中文翻译

从2018年到2021年，大约三年半的时间里，我们一直专注于打磨和改进产品。

我们自主开发了机器人关节的核心零部件，包括谐波减速器、伺服电机、驱动器和控制器。

对于同样大小的关节，我们可以输出更高的扭矩。

在输出相同扭矩的情况下，我们的关节可以做得更加紧凑。

这使我们能够打造出纤细、轻量、外观类似人类手臂的机械臂。

我们的机械臂自重仅7.2千克，但可以提供5千克的负载。

它能够可靠、稳定地运行。

根据我们的测试结果，它达到了5000万小时的MTBF（平均故障间隔时间）。

01

逐句精读

8句全部展开。先听、再读英文，需要时打开中文、发音、语法和词汇。

1. 研发时间

From twenty eighteen to twenty twenty-one, we spent approximately three and a half years refining our products.

翻译：从2018年到2021年，大约三年半的时间里，我们一直专注于打磨和改进产品。

语法：From A to B 表示“从A到B”；spent + 时间 + doing 表示“花费一段时间做某事”；refining 是动名词。

辅助读音：From TWEN-ty eigh-TEEN / to TWEN-ty TWEN-ty-ONE / we SPENT uh-PROK-si-mit-lee three and a HALF years / ri-FINE-ing our PRODUCTS.

单词含义
approximately大约，约等于
spend花费（时间或金钱）
refine改进，打磨

2. 核心零部件自研

We developed the key components of our robotic joints in-house, including harmonic reducers, servo motors, drives, and controllers.

翻译：我们自主开发了机器人关节的核心零部件，包括谐波减速器、伺服电机、驱动器和控制器。

语法：developed 是一般过去时；in-house 作副词，表示“在公司内部、自主地”；including 用来引出具体例子。

辅助读音：we di-VE-ləpt the KEY com-PO-nents / of our ro-BAH-tik JOINTS in-HOUSE / in-KLOO-ding har-MON-ik ri-DOO-sers / SUR-vo MO-tors / DRIVES / and con-TRO-lers.

单词含义
component零部件，组成部分
robotic joint机器人关节
harmonic reducer谐波减速器
servo motor伺服电机
drive驱动器
controller控制器

3. 同样大小，扭矩更高

For the same joint size, we can deliver higher torque.

翻译：对于同样大小的关节，我们可以输出更高的扭矩。

语法：For the same + 名词 表示“在相同的……条件下”；can + 动词原形 表示能力；deliver torque 是“输出扭矩”的专业表达。

辅助读音：for the SAME joint SIZE / we can de-LIV-er HIGH-er TORQUE.

单词含义
same相同的
joint size关节尺寸
deliver提供，输出
torque扭矩，读作接近“tork”

4. 同样扭矩，体积更小

For the same torque output, our joint can be more compact.

翻译：在输出相同扭矩的情况下，我们的关节可以做得更加紧凑。

语法：For the same torque output 是条件背景；can be + 形容词 表示“可以是……的”；more compact 是比较级，表示“更加紧凑”。

辅助读音：for the SAME torque OUT-put / our joint can be more com-PACT.

单词含义
output输出
compact紧凑的，小巧的
more compact更加紧凑

5. 机械臂纤细、小巧

This allows us to build robotic arms that are slim, lightweight, and similar in appearance to a human arm.

翻译：这使我们能够打造出纤细、轻量、外观类似人类手臂的机械臂。

语法：allow sb. to do sth. 表示“使某人能够做某事”；that are... 是定语从句，修饰 robotic arms；similar to 表示“类似于”。

辅助读音：this ə-LOWS us to BUILD ro-BAH-tik ARMS / that are SLIM / LIGHT-weight / and SIM-i-lar in ap-PEAR-ance / to a HUMAN ARM.

单词含义
allow使能够，允许
slim纤细的
lightweight轻量的
similar in appearance to外观类似于

6. 自重和负载

Our robotic arm weighs only seven point two kilograms, but provides a payload of five kilograms.

翻译：我们的机械臂自重仅7.2千克，但可以提供5千克的负载。

语法：weighs 是“重量为……”；only 突出“仅仅”；but 连接重量轻和负载大的对比；a payload of... 表示“……的负载”。

辅助读音：our ro-BAH-tik arm WEIGHS only SEV-en point TWO KIL-o-grams / but pro-VIDES a PAY-load of FIVE KIL-o-grams.

单词含义
weigh重量为
kilogram千克
payload负载，重音在 PAY
provide提供

7. 稳定可靠运行

It can operate reliably and stably.

翻译：它能够可靠、稳定地运行。

语法：can operate 表示“能够运行”；reliably 和 stably 都是副词，说明运行的状态。

辅助读音：it can OP-er-ate ri-LI-a-bly and STAY-bly.

单词含义
operate运行，操作
reliably可靠地
stably稳定地

8. MTBF 测试结果

According to our test results, it has achieved an M T B F of fifty million hours.

翻译：根据我们的测试结果，它达到了5000万小时的MTBF。

语法：According to... 表示“根据……”；has achieved 是现在完成时，强调已经达到的结果；an MTBF of... 表示“……的平均故障间隔时间”。

辅助读音：ac-CORD-ing to our TEST re-SULTS / it has a-CHIEVED an em-tee-bee-EF / of FIFTY MILLION HOURS.

单词含义
according to根据
test result测试结果
achieve达到，实现
MTBFMean Time Between Failures，平均故障间隔时间

对外表达提示：“5000万小时、国内独一份”属于很强的技术和市场声明，正式面对客户时最好按照公司测试报告或官方宣传资料的标准表述。

商务沟通版本

先练30秒版本，再练90秒版本，最后练详细版本。每个版本都可以根据客户的时间和兴趣灵活使用。

约30秒：展会或初次见面

Let me briefly introduce our robotic arm. From 2018 to 2021, we spent three and a half years refining the product. We developed the key joint components in-house, including the harmonic reducer, servo motor, drive, and controller. Our key advantage is high torque density. For the same joint size, we can deliver higher torque, or achieve the same torque in a more compact joint. Our robotic arm weighs 7.2 kilograms and offers a 5-kilogram payload.

参考翻译：我简单介绍一下我们的机械臂。从2018年到2021年，我们用了三年半的时间打磨产品。我们自主开发了关节的核心零部件，包括谐波减速器、伺服电机、驱动器和控制器。我们的核心优势是高扭矩密度。同样大小的关节，我们可以输出更高的扭矩；输出同样的扭矩时，我们的关节可以做得更紧凑。我们的机械臂自重7.2千克，负载达到5千克。

约90秒：正式产品介绍

Let me give you a brief overview of our robotic arm. From 2018 to 2021, we spent approximately three and a half years refining the product. We developed the key components of the robotic joint in-house, including the harmonic reducer, servo motor, drive, and controller. In traditional industrial robots, the controller was often housed in a large external control cabinet. Our integrated joint design helps us build a slimmer and more compact robotic arm. Our key technical advantage is high torque density. For the same joint size, we can deliver higher torque. For the same torque output, our joint can be more compact. This gives customers a lightweight arm that is easier to integrate, especially in applications where space is limited. Our arm weighs only 7.2 kilograms and provides a 5-kilogram payload. It can operate reliably and stably. According to our test results, it has achieved an M T B F of 50 million hours. To understand whether it is suitable for your project, we would like to learn more about your application. What payload and reach do you need? What kind of working space and control interface do you have? Based on your requirements, we can recommend a suitable model and provide more detailed technical information. After the meeting, I can send you the specifications and the next steps.

参考翻译：我简单介绍一下我们的机械臂。2018年至2021年，我们用了大约三年半的时间打磨产品。我们自主开发了机器人关节的核心零部件。传统工业机器人通常需要大型外置控制柜，而我们的集成式关节设计有助于打造更纤细、更紧凑的机械臂。我们的核心技术优势是高扭矩密度，同样大小的关节可以输出更高的扭矩，同样的扭矩可以用更紧凑的关节实现。这让客户得到更轻、更容易集成的机械臂，尤其适合空间有限的应用。机械臂自重仅7.2千克，负载5千克，可以稳定可靠地运行。根据我们的测试结果，MTBF达到5000万小时。为了判断产品是否适合您的项目，我们希望进一步了解您的应用、负载、工作半径、工作空间和控制接口。根据您的需求，我们可以推荐合适的型号并提供更详细的技术资料。

约3分钟：详细技术和需求沟通

Let me explain our product in more detail. From 2018 to 2021, our company spent approximately three and a half years focused on product development and refinement. During this period, we developed the key components of our robotic joints in-house. These components include the harmonic reducer, servo motor, drive, and controller. In traditional industrial robotic arms, the controller was often placed in a large external control cabinet. By developing the joint components as an integrated system, we can optimize the reducer, motor, drive, and controller together. This gives us better control over the joint design and performance. The main technical advantage is high torque density. For a joint of the same size, we can deliver higher torque. Alternatively, for the same torque output, our joint can be designed to be more compact. This compact joint architecture allows us to build robotic arms that are slim, lightweight, and similar in appearance to a human arm. A slimmer arm can be valuable when the robot needs to work in a limited space, close to people, or inside a compact production line. It can also make mechanical integration easier because the arm takes up less space. Our robotic arm weighs only 7.2 kilograms and provides a payload of 5 kilograms. It can operate reliably and stably. According to our test results, the product has achieved an M T B F of 50 million hours. When we evaluate a project, we first look at the application requirements. We would like to know the required payload, reach, working speed, installation direction, and available space. We also need to understand the control method, communication interface, safety requirements, and the working environment. For example, is the robot used for assembly, inspection, handling, or another task? What cycle time do you need? What level of accuracy and repeatability is required? Based on this information, we can recommend an appropriate model and prepare a technical proposal. If some requirements are still unclear, we can first confirm the key parameters and then discuss the next step. After the meeting, I will send you the product specifications, application materials, and a summary of the points we discussed. We can then arrange a technical meeting to review the solution in more detail.

学习提示：这一版用于正式会议。先掌握产品逻辑，再根据公司实际资料补充精度、重复定位精度、通信接口、工作半径、交期和质保等信息。没有确认过的参数不要直接向客户承诺。
